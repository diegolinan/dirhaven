01 / Eligibility

Before we connect.

OpenAI plans and workspace policies determine what ChatGPT can do with a custom MCP server. Your selections here are only guidance, not a live account check.

Your account plan alone does not prove workspace authorization.

Dirhaven cannot inspect your ChatGPT account or bypass its permissions. Check the current official requirements ↗

02 / Authorize

Draw the line.

Dirhaven never assumes a drive, home directory or mount point. The choice is yours.

Or paste an absolute directory path below.

Windows: C:\Users\you\Projects / macOS: /Users/you/Projects / Linux: /home/you/Projects

Broad access is a deliberate choice.

On Windows this covers accessible drive letters, not every UNC share. On Linux and macOS it covers filesystems under /. It won't elevate OS permissions, but an MCP client can request changes to your real files.
